SECTION 01: ECONOMIC PARADIGM

1. The Problem & The Paradigm Shift

The legacy NFT ecosystem is broken. Traditional NFT collections sell static digital images (JPEGs) where founders extract 100% of the capital into private wallets, leaving buyers with illiquid assets that have zero intrinsic value and no computational agency.

THE RARE THESIS
NFTs should not be pictures. They should be autonomous financial actors. By binding non-custodial crypto vaults directly to programmable tokens and seeding them with immediate working capital, we transform passive digital art into a 10,078-agent decentralized algorithmic trading swarm.

2. The 90/10 Self-Funded Capital Routing

In RARE, capital is split atomically at the smart contract level during the single mint transaction. The buyer's SOL is mathematically routed via an Anchor Cross-Program Invocation (CPI):

Key Advantages of 90/10 Split:

  • Built-In Intrinsic Value: Unlike zero-value NFTs, every RARE agent is immediately backed by physical SOL locked inside its own vault.
  • Anti-Rug Architecture: The team cannot drain user funds because 90% never enters team-controlled accounts — it is programmatically locked into the user's agent PDA.
  • Immediate Agent Autonomy: The agent is born fully capitalized, ready to pay its own Solana priority gas fees and execute trades without requiring manual top-ups.

3. Token-Bound Accounts (TBA) on Solana

On Ethereum, this pattern is known as ERC-6551. On Solana, RARE achieves high-performance Token-Bound Accounts through deterministic Program Derived Addresses (PDAs):

PDA Address = Pubkey::find_program_address(&[b"agent-vault", mint_pubkey.as_ref()], &program_id)

Marketplace Transferability: Because the PDA vault is cryptographically bound to the NFT Mint Address, when the NFT is sold on secondary marketplaces (Tensor, Magic Eden), the entire wallet, all accumulated trading profits, and its full transaction history automatically transfer to the new owner.
